Every width.
Both themes.
One capture.

Pick desktop, tablet, mobile or any width between 240 and 3840, in light, dark or both. Each one arrives in Figma as its own frame, and both themes become colour variables with a Light and a Dark mode.

Real layers, not a picture.

Text stays text, in its own fonts. Images stay images, icons become vectors, and flex layouts become Auto Layout where the result lands exactly where the page put it.

Text
Every inline style, link and line break kept as one text layer
Fonts
Matched through each CSS font stack, with every replacement listed
Layout
Auto Layout that is checked against the page, child by child
Styles
Colour variables, text styles, or the styles already in your file
Visuals
Gradients, borders, radii, shadows, blur and SVG as editable vectors
Names
Layers named for what they are, or for their HTML

Behind a login. On staging. On localhost.

The extension captures exactly what your browser shows, so the pages you actually work on are the ones you can bring into Figma: dashboards, previews, a menu left open, a form half filled.

Nothing leaves your computer.

There is no PageRecast server. Captures are built in your browser and kept on your device until you paste them into Figma. No account, no analytics, no limits, and no price.

Open source. Open format.

PageRecast is MIT licensed, and captures are .recast files: gzipped JSON with a documented schema you can read, write and build on.
