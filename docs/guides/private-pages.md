# Pages behind a login, staging and localhost

The extension captures exactly what your browser shows, so anything you can open in Chrome you can capture:

- dashboards and account pages after you sign in
- staging, preview and password-protected environments
- `localhost` and local network addresses while you develop
- pages in a particular state: an open menu, a filled form, a selected tab

Get the page into the state you want, then capture. Nothing is sent anywhere: the capture is built in your browser and stays on your computer until you paste it into Figma.

## Local files

To capture `file://` pages, open `chrome://extensions`, choose **Details** under PageRecast, and turn on **Allow access to file URLs**.
