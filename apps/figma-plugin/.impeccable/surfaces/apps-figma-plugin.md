# Surface brief: PageRecast panels (Figma plugin and Chrome side panel)

Mode: Operate. Visitors are designers mid-task in Figma or Chrome; they repeat this flow many times a day.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PageRecast reads like a native Figma property inspector with one brand-owned stage at the top where the actual page appears. It refuses the wizard of stacked rounded cards, icon-per-row lists, pill tab switchers and monospace "data" costume.

OWN-WORLD: Shades ink #0A0A0B and paper #FDFDFC, hairline full-bleed dividers, 32 px property rows, 6 px radii, Hanken Grotesk 11–13 px with tabular figures, outlined PageRecast wordmark. Red #E0000A only on the single primary action and the selection marker; rose #FF8299 only inside the overlap mark and the stage drawing. No boxes around lists, no eyebrows, no gradient text.

STORY: Paste or capture, see the real page, adjust at most four options, import, read a precise report of what was created and what was substituted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 40 px header (logomark + wordmark left, docs right), text tabs with a 2 px ink underline, then a 176 px stage: empty = drawn page card and Figma layer card overlapping with a ⌘V keycap at the overlap; loaded = the captured screenshot cropped to the top of the page. Inspector sections follow. The primary action is a full-width red button pinned to the bottom.

FORM: Figma inspector plus brand stage; first on the ordered list; no seed key (pinned brand world, code-led, surface inside the Shades world).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
